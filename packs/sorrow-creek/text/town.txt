== intro ==
Ada Pruitt's best mare has bolted, and Ada is in no state to go after her. The
sheriff is asleep. That leaves you.

== main-street ==
Sorrow Creek's only street runs between a handful of false-fronted buildings,
baking in the afternoon sun. The livery stable is east and the sheriff's
office west. A trail drops south into the dry wash below town.

== livery ==
The stable smells of hay and harness oil. Most of the stalls hold drowsing
horses, but the end stall stands open and empty. A wooden peg is driven into
the wall by the door. Main Street is back to the west.

== office ==
A cramped office with a scarred desk, a cold stove and an empty cell at the
back. A poster is tacked to the wall. Main Street is east.

== wash ==
A dry creek bed of cracked clay and white stones, walled by crumbling banks.
Mesquite clings to the edges. The trail climbs north back into town; the wash
itself runs upstream and downstream.

== poster ==
| REWARD
| for the return of a DUN MARE answering to JUNIPER.
| Black stockings, white star.
| Apply A. Pruitt, Livery.

== home ==
Ada takes the rope from you and leads Juniper into the end stall, talking to
her the whole way. When she comes out she presses two silver dollars into your
hand and will not hear a word about it.

The sun goes down red over Sorrow Creek, and for one evening at least, nothing
else goes wrong.

== horse-thief ==
By sundown you are ten miles out and the mare is blown. By morning there is a
poster with your description on the sheriff's wall, and the sheriff is wide
awake.
