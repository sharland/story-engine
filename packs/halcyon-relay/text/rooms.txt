== quarters ==
A narrow cabin with two bunks, one of them yours and one of them empty for as
long as you have been aboard. A storage locker is bolted to the bulkhead. The
hatch to the spine corridor is east.

== spine ==
The station's central corridor, ribbed with conduit. The crew quarters are
west and the galley east. A heavy door to the north leads to the control room,
a ladder well drops down into the maintenance shaft, and the suit room is
south.

== galley ==
A cramped galley with a fold-down table, a water dispenser and the smell of
reheated protein. The stores are behind a door to the east, with a keypad
beside it. The spine corridor is west.

== reactor ==
The squat fusion core hums behind its shielding, still running but cut off
from the rest of the station. A junction panel on the wall has a socket where
the main power coupling belongs, and a breaker lever beside it. Rungs lead up
into the shaft.

== control ==
A horseshoe of consoles faces a window full of slowly turning cloud bands. The
beacon console sits in the middle. The spine corridor is south.

== hull ==
You stand on the station's outer skin, boots locked to the plating. The gas
giant fills half the sky, banded in rust and cream. A maintenance ladder leads
up to the dish mount. The outer hatch is north.
