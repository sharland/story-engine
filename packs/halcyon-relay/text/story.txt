== intro ==
The alarm wakes you, then stops, which is worse.

You are the only crew aboard Halcyon Relay, a communications station in a slow
orbit around the gas giant below. The supply tender Wren is due within hours,
and it cannot dock unless the relay's beacon guides it in. The beacon is
silent. So is everything else.

== slate ==
MAINTENANCE LOG, HALCYON RELAY
| Day 212: replaced filter set 4. Stores code changed after audit: 4471.
| Day 219: dish bearings greased. Spare power coupling logged in stores.
| Day 226: debris warning from Wren. Probably nothing.

== power-on ==
Somewhere below you a relay clacks shut. Fans spin up one after another down
the length of the station, and white light floods back into every room.

== docked ==
For a long minute nothing answers. Then the speakers crackle: "Halcyon, Wren.
We have your beacon. Coming in."

Twenty minutes later the docking clamps close with a sound you feel in your
teeth, and for the first time in two years you hear another human voice in the
corridor.

== vacuum ==
There is no time to be afraid. There is only the cold, and the stars, and the
station turning away beneath you.

== suffocated ==
The tone in your helmet becomes a single long note. The gas giant swims, and
darkens, and goes out.
