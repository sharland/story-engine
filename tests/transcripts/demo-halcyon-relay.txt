## Darkness without the torch; a sealed door until power returns; the drone
The alarm wakes you, then stops, which is worse.
You are the only crew aboard Halcyon Relay, a communications station in a slow orbit around the gas giant below. The supply tender Wren is due within hours, and it cannot dock unless the relay's beacon guides it in. The beacon is silent. So is everything else.
I. Blackout
Crew Quarters
A narrow cabin with two bunks, one of them yours and one of them empty for as long as you have been aboard. A storage locker is bolted to the bulkhead. The hatch to the spine corridor is east. Amber emergency strips glow along the floor.

> e
Spine Corridor
The station's central corridor, ribbed with conduit. The crew quarters are west and the galley east. A heavy door to the north leads to the control room, a ladder well drops down into the maintenance shaft, and the suit room is south. Only the amber emergency strips are lit.
The maintenance drone idles here, its eye light pulsing.

> ask drone about power
"Main power coupling failed during the debris strike," the drone says. "A spare is held in stores."
The maintenance drone rolls away.
